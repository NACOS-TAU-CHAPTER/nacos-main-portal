# Student Networking & Directory - User Guide

**For Students at Thomas Adewumi University NACOS**

---

## Welcome! 🎉

The NACOS Student Dashboard now includes a powerful networking feature that lets you:
- ✨ Create a rich profile showcasing your skills and interests
- 🔍 Discover and connect with other computing students
- 🤝 Share your social media and portfolio links
- 🔒 Control your privacy settings

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Complete Your Profile

1. **Login** to your student dashboard
2. Click the **"Edit Profile"** button
3. Fill in your information:
   - Write a bio about yourself (max 500 characters)
   - Add your skills (e.g., "JavaScript", "Python", "React")
   - Add your interests (e.g., "Web Development", "AI/ML")
   - Link your LinkedIn, GitHub, Twitter, Instagram, or Portfolio
   - Select your year of study and expected graduation year
4. Click **"Save Profile"**

**Tip**: The profile completion bar shows your progress. Aim for 100%! 🎯

---

## Features Explained

### 📝 Edit Your Profile

**Bio**
- Tell other students about yourself
- What are you passionate about?
- What are you working on?
- Keep it under 500 characters

**Skills**
- Add programming languages you know (e.g., "JavaScript", "Python")
- Add technologies you use (e.g., "React", "Node.js", "Docker")
- Add tools you're proficient in (e.g., "Git", "VS Code")
- Press **Enter** to add each skill
- Click the **X** to remove a skill
- Maximum: 20 skills

**Interests**
- What areas of tech interest you? (e.g., "Web Development", "AI/ML")
- What do you want to learn? (e.g., "Blockchain", "Mobile Dev")
- Press **Enter** to add each interest
- Click the **X** to remove an interest
- Maximum: 10 interests

**Social Media & Links**
- **LinkedIn**: Your professional profile
- **GitHub**: Your code repositories
- **Twitter/X**: Your tech Twitter
- **Instagram**: Your Instagram (if you share tech content)
- **Portfolio**: Your personal website or portfolio

**Academic Info**
- **Year of Study**: 100L, 200L, 300L, 400L, or 500L
- **Graduation Year**: When you expect to graduate

---

### 🔒 Privacy Settings

Control who can see your information:

**Profile Visibility**
- **Students Only** (Recommended): All NACOS students can see your profile
- **Private**: Only you can see your profile (you won't appear in the directory)

**Field Visibility**
- **Show my email**: Let others contact you via email
- **Show my phone**: Share your phone number with other students
- **Show my matric number**: Display your matric number on your profile

**Default**: All privacy settings start in the most restrictive mode for your safety.

---

### 🔍 Student Directory

Find and connect with other students:

**How to Access**
1. From your dashboard, click **"Student Directory"** in the Quick Access section
2. The directory will load with all active students

**Search**
- Type a student's name in the search box
- Results update automatically as you type

**Filters**
- **Department**: Filter by Computer Science, Software Engineering, etc.
- **Year**: Filter by 100L, 200L, 300L, 400L, 500L
- **Sort**: Sort by Name (A-Z, Z-A) or Recently Joined

**Tips**:
- Use filters together for precise results
- Click **"Clear"** to reset all filters
- Use pagination to browse through pages

### 👤 View Student Profiles

**Profile View**

## Privacy Best Practices

### What to Share
✅ **Safe to Share**:
- Your bio and interests
- Your skills and year of study
- Your social media profiles (they're already public)
- Your portfolio website
- Your LinkedIn profile

### What to Be Careful With
⚠️ **Be Cautious**:
- Your personal phone number (use privacy toggle)
- Your personal email (use privacy toggle if concerned)
- Your exact location or address (don't put in bio)

### Recommended Settings
For most students, we recommend:
- **Profile Visibility**: Students Only
- **Show Email**: ON (it's your school email, safe to share with classmates)
- **Show Phone**: OFF (unless you want calls/texts)
- **Show Matric**: ON (helps classmates identify you)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I edit my profile later?
Yes! Click "Edit Profile" anytime to update your information.

### Will everyone see my profile?
Only if you set visibility to "Students Only". If you set it to "Private", only you can see it (you won't appear in the directory).

### Can I remove a skill or interest?
Yes! In the edit profile form, click the X next to any tag to remove it.

### What if I don't have a LinkedIn or GitHub?
That's fine! All social media fields are optional. We recommend creating them though—they're valuable for your career!

### How do I make my email private?
In Edit Profile → Privacy Settings → Toggle "Show my email" to OFF.

### Can I see who viewed my profile?
Not yet, but this feature may be added in the future!

### Why can't I add more than 20 skills?
We limit it to keep profiles focused on your core competencies. Choose your best 20 skills!

### I found a bug or have a suggestion. Who do I contact?
Contact the NACOS Tech Team or your class representatives.
